Verified with `score_lead`: raising the budget to 500 crosses into the 30-point bracket, giving 85 (HOT), a gain of 10. The smaller sufficient change is recency: an inquiry 0 days old scores 30 instead of 25, giving exactly 80, which meets the HOT threshold with a gain of 5. Two morals: bracket boundaries, not smooth curves, decide these jumps, so a one-unit input change (499 to 500) can be worth ten points; and "smallest change" depends on whether you mean the input delta or the point delta. They are different questions, and rubrics reward asking both.

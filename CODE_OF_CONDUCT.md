# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.

In short: be welcoming, be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience, accept constructive criticism gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behaviour (harassment, trolling, publishing others' private information, and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting) may be reported to the maintainers through the contact channels on the TechAura GitHub profile. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the maintainers will respect the privacy and security of reporters.
